Herdr’s TUI is great.
Here’s what a window adds.

Herdr GPUI is a native client for the Herdr daemon you already run. Your terminals and agents stay in the daemon; the window adds dispatch from issues, side-by-side review, checkpoints, Teleport and a Devices overview.
